In-Game Replay


Posted By: Jeff Fisher
January 2, 2025


	
		
Jack Muldoon, who led Monsignor Bonner/Archbishop Prendergast to the school's first-ever high school football state championships is the Pennsylvania Football Writers' Class 4A All-State high school football team. The title led B-P to a No. 202 ranking in the High School Football America 300 national rankings, powered by NFL Play Football.




The 4A Player of the Year is Caileb Howse, who led Lampeter-Strasburg to a runner-up finish behind Bonner-Prende as a first-year starter at QB. Howse threw for 1,610 yards with 18 touchdown. He also ran for 1,444 yards and 20 scores.
