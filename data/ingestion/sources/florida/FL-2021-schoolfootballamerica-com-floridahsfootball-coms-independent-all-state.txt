		TOPICS:All-State High School Football TeamFlorida High School FootballFlorida high school football newsFloridaHSFootball.com
	
	

Miami Gardens TRU Prep Academy came together during the COVID-19 pandemic to allow those who missed the chance of playing for some schools that shut down football for the season. A lot of the best performers from the team are represented on the 2020 Independent All-State Football Team [Photo Courtesy of TRU Prep Academy]


In-Game Replay


Posted By: Jeff Fisher
April 8, 2021


	
		
The below story is from our friends at FloridaHSFootball.com




Here are the 2020 Independent All-State Football Teams compiled by FloridaHSFotball.com Publisher Joshua Wilson along with the consideration of information received from coaches, statistics on MaxPreps, and consultation of all-area/all-county teams as published by various media outlets from around the state. Stats, placements in all-area/all-county teams as well as schedule played by a respected school were all considered factors for the All-State Teams, but ever so gently given the various amount of games schools played this season across the state due to the COVID-19 pandemic.

			Jeff is an award-winning journalist and expert in the field of high school sports, underscored with his appearance on CNBC in 2010 to talk about the big business of high school football in America.

Jeff turned to his passion for high school football into an entrepreneurial venture called High School Football America, a digital media company focused on producing original high school sports content for radio, television and the internet.

Jeff is co-founder and editor-in-chief of High School Football America, a partner with NFL Play Football.
