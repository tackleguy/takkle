 OSAA All-State: Here are the 2025 football teams 

First, second, third and honorable mentions for all classifications for football in 2025

 December 19, 2025 by Austin White, OSAAtoday 

 

To close out the 2025 Oregon high school fall sports season, it's time for the OSAA All-State football teams to be announced.

Teams, Players of the Year and Coaches of the Year were selected based on voting from coaches around the state. Coaches were required to vote in order for their players to be eligible for the All-State teams.

You can also see the full results and keep track of all the OSAA All-State teams by following the link here.
