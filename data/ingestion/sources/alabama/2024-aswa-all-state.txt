Class 7A champ Thompson and four from 6A champ Parker were selected to the first team in the Alabama Sports Writers Association annual All-State football squads for 2024.

The ASWA’s prep committee selected a first team, second team and up to two honorable mention players at each position from nominations submitted by statewide high school football coaches.

Thompson’s first team selections were QB Trent Seaborn, DB Anquon Fegans, DL Jared Smith, OL Barron Criddle and P John McGuire.

Parker’s first-team selections were all on the Herd’s talented defense – a pair of DL in Jourdin Crawford and Robert Taylor III and a pair of DBs in Na’eem Offord and Timothy Merritt. 

Three finalists in each AHSAA classification and the AISA will be announced on Christmas Day. The winners and Mr. Football will be announced at the annual Player of the Year Banquet in Montgomery on Jan. 28.
