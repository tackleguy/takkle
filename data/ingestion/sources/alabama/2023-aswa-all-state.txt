A pair of junior quarterbacks were first-team All-State picks in the state’s two largest classifications.

The Alabama Sports Writers Association released its 2023 All-State football team on Saturday morning.

Andrew Alford, who led Central-Phenix City, to the state championship was Class 7A’s first-team quarterback. Saraland’s KJ Lacey, a Texas commit, was first team in Class 6A for the second straight season.

Other high-profile stars who made first team include Central-Phenix City wide receiver Cam Coleman, Foley’s Perry Thompson, Saraland’s Ryan Williams, Clay-Chalkville’s Jaylen Mbakwe, Gulf Shores’ Ronnie Royal and Elba’s Alvin Henderson.

The All-State team came from nominations from statewide coaches to their respective sports writers and was voted on by the ASWA’s prep committee. Players must be nominated by their coaches to be considered.

On Wednesday, the ASWA will release three finalists for Back and Lineman of the Year in each of the seven AHSAA classifications as well as the AISA.

The backs and linemen of the year – along with the state’s Mr. Football – will be announced Jan. 16 at a banquet in Montgomery.
