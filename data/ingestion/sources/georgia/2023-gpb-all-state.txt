        The 2023 GPB All-State All-Star Team is a collaborative effort of our GPB Sports staff who work Football Fridays in Georgia. This is an all-classification honors team of the top GHSA football seniors in state.

Most Outstanding Two-Way Player 

Sammy Brown – Jefferson – 6-3, 230, Senior

Season Stats: On offense rushed 255 times for 2,289 yards (3rd in GHSA), 9.0 average, 163.5 yards per game, 11 100-plus yard rushing games; 11 receptions for 202 yards with 40 total touchdowns. On defense 153 total tackles, 12 tackles for loss, one QB sack, two interceptions. Jefferson career: 4491 rushing yards, 75 Total touchdowns, 247 total tackles. Recruiting: Signed with Clemson over twenty-seven other offers. Highest Ranking: ESPN 5-Star and rated the No. 1 LB in the country in the Class of 2024, the No. 12 overall prospect in the nation and No. 3 in Georgia. Team: 13-1 reached semifinals of 5A playoffs and finished No. 3 in final GPB Sports Final 5A Top 10 Rankings. 

Most Outstanding Offensive Player 

Jeremy Hecklinski – Walton – 6-1, 180, Senior 

Season Stats: 212-for- 333 passing (63.7 percent) for 3,835 yards (No. 2 GHSA), 255.7 yards per game) 50 touchdowns and just six interceptions. Career: 9,897 yards passing with 107 touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with Wake Forest over three other offers. Consensus 3-Star. Team: 14-1. 7A runner-up. Ranked No. 2 in Final GPB Sports 7A Top 10.  

Most Outstanding Defensive Player 

K.J. Bolden – Buford – 6-0, 185, Senior 

Season: 33 total tackles with four interceptions, one touchdown. Played both sides of the ball with 30 receptions for 633 yards and 14 touchdowns. Also had two kick return touchdowns and scored 17 total touchdowns for the season. Recruiting: Signed with Georgia flipping from his commitment to Florida State. Consensus 5-Star and No.1 safety in the country. Team: 11-2, reached the 7A quarterfinals and finished No. 8 in Final GPB Sports 7A Top 10. 

Most Outstanding Coach of the Year

Kevin Smith – Perry Panthers

Led team to first state championship in the program’s 70-year history, the longest that any GHSA team had gone without a title. Smith has compiled a 60-26 record in seven seasons with Perry after 24 years as an assistant coach before getting his first head coaching job. Panthers are 34-6 the last three seasons after going 26-20 in his first four seasons. Three region crowns.

Click here to see the Offensive team

Quarterbacks 

Dylan Raiola – Buford - 6-3, 230, Senior 

Stats: 160-for-253 passing (63.2 percent), 2,666 yards (205.1 yards per game) with 34 touchdowns, just one interception. Career (including Arizona HS stats) 8,442 passing yards and 88 touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with Nebraska after flipping from Georgia Dec. 18, less than two days before the start of Early NSD period. Highest ranking: 247 Sports and Rivals rated him 5-Star and No. 1 QB in the country and No. 2 overall prospect in the country. Team: 11-2, reached the 7A quarterfinals and finished No. 8 in Final GPB Sports 7A Top 10. 

Luke Kromenhoek – Benedictine - 6-4, 185, Senior 

Stats: 168-for-267 passing (62.9 percent), 2,578 yards (184.1 yards per game) with 24 touchdowns, two interceptions, 558 rushing yards with seven touchdowns. Career: 5,277 passing yards and 48 touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with Florida State over eleven other offers. Consensus 4-Star and ranked as high as No. 3 QB in the country by On3 Recruits. Team: 13-1, reached 4A semifinal and finished No. 3 in Final GPB Sports 4A Top 10.

Jake Merklinger – Calvary Day – 6-3, 195, Senior 

Stats: 163-for-226 passing (72.1 percent), 3,028 yards (232.9 yards per game) with 38 touchdowns, two interceptions. 439 yards rushing and 11 touchdowns. Career: 9635 yards passing with 113 touchdowns. 1,129 yards rushing with 31 touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with Tennessee over 28 other offers. Consensus 4-Star and rated as high as No. 6 QB in the country by Rivals. Team: 13-1, reached 3A semifinals and finished No. 3 in Final GPB Sports 3A Top 10.

Aaron Philo – Prince Avenue - 6-2, 200, Senior

Stats: 328-for-469 passing (69.9 percent), 4,596 yards (No. 1 GHSA) 328.3 yards per game with 56 touchdowns (tied the state’s single season record), nine interceptions. Rushed for 610 yards and 15 touchdowns. Career: Set the state’s career passing yards record with 13,922 yards, a record previously held by Trevor Lawrence with 159 touchdowns (finished third on that career list). Had 1,200 career rushing yards and 32 touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with Georgia Tech over 14 other offers. Consensus 3-Star. Notes: Second time named GPB All-State and two-time GPB Sports All-Finals Team including 2023 All-Finals Most Outstanding Offensive Player. Team: 14-0 and Class A Division I champions for the second straight season and third time in 4 years.

Running Backs 




Fred Brown – Coffee – 5-10, 200, Senior 

Stats: Rushed 358 times 2,488 yards (led GHSA), 7.0 average, 165.9 yards per game, 15 100-plus yard rushing games, 24 touchdowns. Career: 3,809 yards rushing and 45 total touchdowns. Team: 15-0 and 5A champions for the first time in program history.

Mason Sullens – Lumpkin County – 6-0, 215, Senior 

Stats: Rushed 332 times for 2,281 rushing yards (4th GHSA), 6.9 average, 175.5 yards per game, 13 100-plus yard rushing games, 33 touchdowns. Career: 3,356 yards rushing and 51 total touchdowns. Team: 12-1 reached the quarterfinals of the Class 3A playoffs and finished No. 6 in the Final GPB Sports 3A Top 10.

Quinton Slaughter – Providence Christian – 5-9, 170, Senior 

Stats: Rushed 235 times for 2,202 yards (5th GHSA), 8.7 average, 200.2 yards per game, 10 100-plus yard rushing games, 22 touchdowns. Recruiting: Offers include Shorter, Berry and Sewanee. Team: 8-3 program’s best-ever record and reached GHSA playoffs for the first time in program history.

Matthew Fuller – Wayne County – 6-0, 210, Senior 

Stats: Rushed 244 times for 1,873 yards rushing, 7.7 average, 170.7 yards per game, nine 100-plus yard rushing games, 24 touchdowns. Career: 3,630 yards rushing and 47 total touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with South Carolina over 22 other offers. Highest Ranking On3 Recruits 4-Star and No. 11 RB in the country. Team: 7-4 reaching state playoffs for second straight season following an 0-9 record in 2021.

Jayden “Duke” Scott – Stockbridge – 5-10, 200, Senior 

Stats: Rushed 237 times for 2,482 rushing yards (No. 2 GHSA), 10.5 average, 165.5 yards per game, 13 100-plus yard rushing games, 40 total touchdowns. Career: 3,483 yards and 56 total touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with NC State over 26 other offers. Consensus 3-Star. Team: 12-3 and Class 4A runner-up reaching state championship game for first time in program’s 60-year history. Finished No. 2 in the Final GPB Sports 4A Top 10.

Duke Watson – Mary Persons – 6-0, 180, Senior 

Stats: Rushed 220 times for 1,865 rushing yards, 8.5 average, 169.5 yards per game, 10 100-plus yard rushing games, 30 touchdowns. Career: 5,541 yards rushing 74 Total touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with Louisville over 21 other offers. Highest Ranking On3 Recruits 4-Star and No. 22 RB in the country. Team: 11-2 reaching Class 3A quarterfinals and finished No. 5 in the Final GPB Sports 3A Top 10.

Roderick McCrary – Creekside – 5-10, 190, Senior 

Stats: Rushed 234 times for 1,959 yards, 8.4 average, 139.9 yards per game, 13 100-plus yard rushing games, 16 total touchdowns.  Career: 3,411 yards, 33 touchdowns. Recruiting: Has 23 offers including Georgia Tech, Florida State and Old Dominion. Highest Ranking Rivals 3-Star RB. Team: 13-2 and finishing as 5A state runner-up.

Devin Ingram – Stephenson – 5-9, 175, Senior

Stats: 158-1746 rushing yards, 11.1 average, 194.0 yards per game, eight 100-plus yard rushing games and 25 total touchdowns. Career: 3,094 rushing yards and 44 Total touchdowns. Recruiting: Offers include Georgia Military College, ETSU, Southeast Missouri State. Team: 10-3 reaching quarterfinals of Class 4A playoffs and finishing No. 9 in Final GPB Sports 4A Top 10 rankings.

Wide Receivers 

Mike Matthews – Parkview - 6-1, 180, Senior

Stats: 39 receptions for 751 yards, 19.3 average, 68.3 yards per game, nine touchdowns. Career: 135 receptions for 2,535 receiving yards, 25 touchdowns, Recruiting: Signed with Tennessee over 32 other offers and already bowl practicing with the Vols ahead of NSD. Highest Ranking ESPN 5-Star and No. 3 WR in the country, No. 9 overall prospect and No. 3 recruit in Georgia. Team: 7-4 reaching the state playoffs for the ninth consecutive season.

Sacovie White – Cass – 5-8, 170, Senior 

Recruiting: Signed with Georgia over four other offers. Highest Ranking Rivals 4-Star

Team: 7-6 reaching the quarterfinals of the State 5A playoffs in the program’s deepest playoff run since 1983. Finished 10th in the Final GPB Sports 5A Top 10.

Debron Gatling – Milton 6-0, 180, Senior 

Stats: 77 receptions for 892 yards, 11.6 average, 63.7 yards per game, 11 touchdowns. Career: 204 receptions for 2,816 receiving yards and 33 touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with South Carolina after decommitting from Texas A&M during state  championship week. Consensus 3-Star. Team: 13-2 and 7A State Champions for the second time in program history.

Tyler Stewart – Jones County – 5-9, 170, Senior 

Stats: 59 receptions for 1,265 yards, 21.4 average, 105.4 yards per game, 21 touchdowns. Career: 114 receptions for 2,034 yards and 31 touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with Temple over eight other offers. Highest Ranking ESPN 3-Star. Team: 8-4 reaching second round of the GHSA 5A Playoffs.

Isaiah Canion – Warner Robins – 6-3, 190, Senior 

Recruiting: Signed with Georgia Tech over 16 other offers including Notre Dame to whom he originally committed. Consensus 4-Star and rated as high as No. 12 WR in the country by ESPN. Team: 9-4 reaching the Class 5A quarterfinals, breaking a streak of five straight state championship game appearances (won two of those). Canion led the Demons to the 2022 finals as the starting QB stepping in when then the No. 1 went down with injury,

Judson Petty – North Murray – 5-10, 175, Senior 

Stats: 74 receptions for 1,407 yards (No. 2 GHSA), 19.0 average, 117.3 yards per game, 25 total touchdowns. Also had 518 yards rushing. 2,396 all-purpose yards. Set the state single-game receiving record with 16 catches for 360 yards and four touchdowns in the 44-37 win over Pickens. Recruiting: Offers include Point University.

Team: 8-4 reaching the second round of the Class 2A playoffs.

Javarius George – North Paulding – 5-10, 173, Senior 

Stats: 51 receptions for 1,202 yards, 23.6 average, 100.2 yards per game, eight touchdowns. Career: 70 receptions for 1,661 yards and 11 touchdowns. Recruiting: Has 14 offers including Middle Tennessee, Western Kentucky, and Coastal Carolina. Team: 9-3 reaching the second round of the Class 7A playoffs.

Ny Carr – Colquitt County – 5-11, 168, Senior 

Stats: 64 receptions for 1,261 yards receiving, 19.7 average, 97.0 yards per game, 21 touchdowns. Career: 169-3,195 yards and 45 total touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with Miami over 26 other offers including Georgia to whom he was originally committed. Consensus 4-Star. Highest Ranking Rivals No. 8 WR in the country and No. 7 prospect in Georgia. Team: 12-1 reaching the 7A quarterfinals before losing 39-37 at eventual state champion Milton. Finished No. 7 in the Final GPB Sports 7A Top 10.

Tight Ends 

Walter Matthews – Hiram – 6-7, 245, Senior 

Stats: 25 receptions for 341 yards, 13.6 average, 1 touchdowns. Career: 57-1,001 yards, 11 touchdowns. Recruiting: Signed with Southern Cal over 29 other offers. Consensus 4-Star and rated as high as the No. 2 TE in the country by ESPN.

Team: 9-3 reaching second round of the Class 5A playoffs in the program’s best season since 2010. Finished No. 8 in the final GPB Sports 5A Top 10.

Caleb Odom – Carrollton – 6-5, 215, Senior
