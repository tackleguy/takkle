        The 2025 GPB Sports All-Class, All-State Team is a collaborative effort of GPB Sports Football Fridays in Georgia. This is an all-classification honors team of the top GHSA football players in state.

Most Outstanding Offensive Player 

Kaiden Prothro – Bowdon – 6-6, 210, Senior TE/WR






  









Season Stats: 62 receptions for 1,186 yards with 28 TDs. Career Stats: 161 receptions for 3,436 yards receiving with 67 TDs, setting the GHSA career TD receptions record while leading Bowdon to four straight state championships. Recruiting: 5-Star rated as high as No. 1 TE in the country 2026. Signed with Georgia over 38 other offers. Team: 13-2. Class 1AD2 championship.  

Most Outstanding Defensive Player 

Deuce Geralds – Collins Hill – 6-2, 260, Senior DE 

Season Stats: 91 total tackles, 37 tackles for loss, 16.5 sacks, 45 QB hurries, 7 forced fumbles. Career Stats: 352 total tackles, 114.5 tackles for loss, 54 sacks, 135 hurries. 

Offense: 533 yards rushing with 15 TDs. Recruiting: 4-Star rated as high as No. 2 DL in the country.. Signed with LSU over 54 other offers. Honors: Back-to-back GPB Sports All-State (2024-25). Team: 6-5. Reached first round 6A playoffs.

Most Outstanding Coach of the Year

Korey Mobbs – Jackson County





              






    Credit:      Jackson County Panthers High School Football Facebook Page/ Jennifer Wiley Photography












The veteran head coach of 12 seasons led Jackson County to easily the best season ever in the Panthers’ 46-year program history. Jackson County finished 11-2 in a season of firsts: First ever quarterfinal playoff appearance. First two playoff wins in program history. First ever season of double-digit digit wins and first ever region championship. En route to the Panthers first ever playoff wins, Jackson County eliminated two-time defending state champion Milton in the second round of the playoffs. Jackson County won the Region 8-5A title and nine of the Panthers 11 wins were by three touchdowns or more.
